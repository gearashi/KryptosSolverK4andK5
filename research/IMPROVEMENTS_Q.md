# Phase Q: all 90 proposal outcomes

All 90 requested proposals were implemented and evaluated in bounded controls. **89 remain available as optional methods, conditional models or supporting primitives. The restart portfolio (60) is disabled after its negative comparison.** This does not mean 89 independent improvements in search decisions have been established. Several methods tie a previous method, trade fewer decisions for more checking, or add a useful model without a measured primary-work win.

The acceptance criterion is the user's definition: fewer search decisions or completed candidate evaluations to the same declared correct result. Wall-clock speed alone does not qualify. Preparation, factor lookups, graph transitions, probes, proof verification and literal replay are recorded separately. No authenticated K4/K5 decipherment has been produced.

The [initial numbered plan](phase_Q_plan_initial.json) is preserved. The [current machine-readable ledger](phase_Q_plan.json) connects every number to code, scope, decision and evidence; the same ledger appears in the browser's Research methods panel.

## What the measurements establish

- Verified subtree reuse reduces 21 new decisions to 3 on its fixed control. Including proof creation and verification, counted work is 157 versus 80, and complete objective work is 12 versus 11.
- A bounded overlapping-family bank scores 702 distinct plaintexts instead of 729 separate family assignments; all 2,185 internal and final literal replays are charged.
- Exact junction and chain alternatives tie the previous `auto` decomposition on primary decisions in 33 controls. Their new conditional-query reuse facilities are retained without claiming a new one-shot search win.
- Cycle bounds improve over the static overlap bound on six controls without worsening that primary count in the frozen suite. Other bound/order choices have both favorable and unfavorable results.
- A geometric 3x4 control drops from 33 to 12 decisions with degree/connectivity constraints. This assumes a neighbor-respecting path; it does not establish Kryptos uses one.
- The finite codebook control reduces 927 complete tokenizations to 60 distinct outputs. Its ENE/BC interpretation remains an explicit hypothesis and does not replace the literal released K4 clues.
- Six binary recipe slots preserve all64 syntactic recipes using12 symbolic transitions instead of126 literal prefix edges. Algebraic canonicalization also costs work. Counterexample-guided synthesis halves equation rows on its control but adds key proposals/replays, so that outcome is mixed.
- The restarted portfolio completes only3 of24 bounded cases; all three use more decisions and complete evaluations than its baseline. Its active dispatcher now rejects it.

## Evidence, integration and limits

The main audits are [constraints](runs/release-Q-001/constraints/manifest.json), [factors](runs/release-Q-002/factor/manifest.json), [geometry/language models](runs/release-Q-models-003/manifest.json), and [grammar work](runs/release-Q-grammar-001/manifest.json). All use bounded execution and version2 manifests. Independent reviews cover [constraint relations](review_Q_constraints.md), [factor edge cases](review_Q_factors_from_constraints.md), [recipe semantics](review_Q_grammar.md), and [basis changes/coverage proofs](review_Q_basis_coverage.md). A passing finite audit is not a universal proof of the implementation.

The factor audit contains561 exact integrated comparisons. The geometry tests cover63 nonempty2x3 and511 nonempty3x3 subgraphs with independent literal oracles. The independent grammar review checks80 shared-key recipe banks against36,414 exhaustive key vectors. The basis/coverage review adds1,152 affine set comparisons,19 subtree reuse checks and24 tampered-proof rejections.

Preserved unsuccessful runs include the first factor manifest with `inputs-changed`, the period-27 hardware fixture that could not provide the requested distinct range, older model/grammar revisions, and the negative portfolio results. They are not substituted for successful final records. The constraint audit's conservative whole-source snapshot predates disabling the unrelated factor portfolio dispatch; the constraint dependency path is unchanged and the final package suite checks the integrated release.

The [research-method guide](../docs/RESEARCH_METHODS.md) explains the four browser/CLI engines, real-cipher adapters, strategy selectors, optional SMT dependency and saved technique traces. Finite assignments and geometry witnesses remain distinct from plaintext candidates. Every returned cipher candidate has its original method, assumptions and replay evidence.

The [hardware guide](../docs/HARDWARE_SETTINGS.md) records the actual8-thread CPU and RTX2060 tests. Hardware throughput is a separate engineering result, not an algorithmic search reduction. [Release validation](release-validation.json) records packaged tests, browser checks and publication status.

This completes the requested90-item batch; it does not exhaust possible future cryptanalysis. Large or unsupported models can hit an explicit resource cap, which remains inconclusive. K5 input, physical registration, codebooks, language dictionaries and grammar transfer are supplied assumptions unless separately authenticated.
